Our mission

Trading without context is just guessing.

The professionals don't lose because their charts are better — they win because they trade with context. A live news feed scored by analysts. A research team interpreting central-bank speak in seconds. A geopolitical desk pricing every escalation. Real-time order-flow telling them where the liquidity is.

Retail traders get none of that. They get a chart, a candlestick pattern, and a Discord room full of opinions. They lose, repeatedly, to the structural information gap.

TradeContext.ai exists to close that gap. Every feature on this platform — the AI news bias, the geopolitical risk meter, the macro pulse, the AI analyst chat — exists to give one trader, sitting at one screen, the same situational awareness a Goldman desk has at 7am.
