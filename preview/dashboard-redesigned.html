Frequently Asked Questions?
Everything you need to know about TradeContext.ai. Still have questions? Email us →
What does TradeContext.ai actually do?+
We're an AI trading intelligence platform. The dashboard scores every breaking news story for bias, runs live fundamental + technical analysis on every symbol, surfaces macro flow (currency strength, risk sentiment, fear & greed, sessions, world pulse, vol gauges), and gives you a per-chart AI chat that answers "why is this moving right now?" — all in seconds, all grounded in live data. We're not a broker. We never place trades. We hand you the highest-conviction setup; you execute on your regulated platform.
Is there a free trial?+
No free trial in the traditional sense — but you get a 2-minute free preview of the live dashboard, news feed and AI bias engine before the paywall kicks in. That's enough to feel the speed and depth. After that it's $39.95/mo, $399/yr (save 17%), or $799 lifetime (first 500 customers only). 14-day money-back guarantee on every plan.
How much does it cost? Are there tiers?+
One plan, no tiers. Every feature for every customer. $39.95/month billed monthly, $399/year (~$33/mo, save 17%), or a one-time $799 lifetime for early supporters — pay once, never billed again. We deliberately avoided the "upgrade for the good stuff" pricing trap.
How does the AI news bias actually work?+
Every breaking story is fed to Claude (Anthropic's flagship model) within seconds of publication. Claude reads the headline, body and reasoning, then outputs a bias (bullish/bearish/neutral), an impact score (high/medium/low), and the affected assets. We log every call so you can see the public hit-rate on our Track Record page.
Which brokers can I use?+
58 regulated brokers worldwide are listed in the "Open in [Broker]" dropdown — OANDA, IG, Pepperstone, IC Markets, Vantage, Saxo, Interactive Brokers, eToro, Coinbase, Kraken, and many more. We're also fully compatible with MT4 / MT5 (one-click launcher copies your setup to clipboard, then opens the platform). We're broker-agnostic — pick the regulated one that suits you.
Do you ever execute trades for me?+
No. We're not regulated as a broker and never want to be. Our job is to give you the highest-conviction context — your job is to execute on a regulated broker. The "Copy Setup" button puts the order details on your clipboard so you can paste them into your broker's New Order box. (We do publish a free MT4/5 Expert Advisor that traders can run on their own MetaTrader to auto-execute signals — that's an optional install, your platform, your trades.)
Can I use this for prop firm challenges (FTMO, TopStep)?+
Yes — that's actually one of our core use-cases. Live drawdown thermometer with audible alerts at 50/70/90% of your daily-loss limit, breaking-news guard so you flatten before red-folder events, AI mistake auto-tagger that finds repeat patterns in your journal. See our Prop Firm page for the full playbook.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes — one click from your account settings. No lock-ins, no clawback, no questions. We also offer a 14-day money-back guarantee — if it's not for you in the first 14 days, email hello@tradecontext.ai and we'll refund you in full.
Is my data secure?+
Auth tokens live in httpOnly cookies, passwords are bcrypt-hashed, and any broker API credentials we hold are AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest with a server-side key. All broker integrations are read-only — we can never move funds. We don't sell or share your data, ever.
Who's behind TradeContext.ai?+
A solo founder in London (hi 👋). Bootstrapped, no VC, no growth-hack tactics — just a tool I wanted to use, opened up to anyone who wants the same edge. Direct line: hello@tradecontext.ai. More about us →
Is the AI ever wrong?+
Yes — sometimes. AI bias models are probabilistic; even a 65% hit-rate engine misses 35% of calls. That's why we publish a verified track record with every call permanent the moment it's generated, no cherry-picking. Treat every output as a high-quality data point, not gospel. Always size with risk in mind.
