FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does TradeContext.ai actually do? +
We're an AI trading intelligence platform. We score every breaking news story for bias, run live fundamental + technical analysis on every symbol, surface macro flow (currency strength, risk sentiment, fear & greed, sessions, world pulse, vol gauges, geopolitical risk), and give you a per-chart AI chat that answers "why is this moving right now?" — all in seconds, all grounded in live data. We're not a broker. We never place trades. We hand you the highest-conviction setup; you execute on your regulated platform.
How much does it cost? Are there tiers? +
One plan, no tiers. Every feature for every customer. $39.95/month billed monthly · $399/year (~$33/mo, save 17%) · or a one-time $799 lifetime for early supporters. 14-day money-back guarantee on every plan.
Is the AI ever wrong? +
Yes — sometimes. AI bias models are probabilistic; even a 68% hit-rate engine misses 32% of calls. That's why we publish a verified track record with every call permanent the moment it's generated, no cherry-picking. Treat every output as a high-quality data point, not gospel. Always size with risk in mind.
Do you ever execute trades for me? +
No. We're not regulated as a broker and never want to be. Our job is to give you the highest-conviction context — your job is to execute on a regulated broker. The "Copy Setup" button puts the order details on your clipboard so you can paste them into your broker's New Order box.
Can I use this for prop firm challenges? +
Yes — that's one of our core use cases. Live drawdown thermometer with audible alerts at 50/70/90% of your daily-loss limit, breaking-news guard so you flatten before red-folder events, AI mistake auto-tagger that finds repeat patterns. Works for FTMO, TopStep, The5%ers, FundedNext, MFF.
Which brokers can I use? +
58 regulated brokers — OANDA, IG, Pepperstone, IC Markets, Vantage, Saxo, Interactive Brokers, eToro, Coinbase, Kraken, and many more. We're also fully compatible with MT4 / MT5. We're broker-agnostic — pick the regulated one that suits you.
Is my data secure? +
Auth tokens in httpOnly cookies. Passwords bcrypt-hashed. Any broker API credentials we hold are AES-256-GCM encrypted. All broker integrations read-only — we can never move funds. GDPR compliant. We don't sell or share your data.
