SECTION 02

Risk of loss

Trading financial markets is high-risk. The instruments most commonly traded by our users — CFDs, foreign exchange (FX), futures, options and crypto-assets — are leveraged or volatile instruments. Some carry the risk of losses exceeding your deposit.

You could lose all of your invested capital — and in some cases more. Trading is not suitable for everyone. Only trade with money you can afford to lose completely. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

Industry-published statistics suggest that between 70% and 85% of retail CFD accounts lose money on a typical broker. Specific figures are published by each regulated broker on their website.

SECTION 04

AI output — what it can and can't do

TradeContext.ai uses large language models (currently Anthropic's Claude) to interpret news headlines, generate trade context, and coach users on their journal. These models are statistical — they predict plausible-sounding next words based on patterns in their training data.

That means:

  • AI output can be wrong. Hallucinated levels, mis-interpreted news, missed context, outdated information — all possible.
  • AI output is not a forecast. A bullish bias score on a headline does not mean the market will rise. It means our model assessed the sentiment of the headline as bullish.
  • AI output has no liability. Following an AI suggestion does not transfer responsibility — every trade decision and outcome is yours.

We log every AI bias call we publish on our public Track Record page (when available) so accuracy is visible — but historical hit rate does not predict future hit rate.

SECTION 07

Market data & accuracy

Prices and trade history shown on TradeContext.ai are sourced from third-party data providers — currently Yahoo Finance (equities · forex · commodities · indices), Binance (crypto spot + perpetual prices), Bybit, and Finnhub (news + US equities). We refresh prices every 5 seconds, but data is provided "as-is" and is not guaranteed to be tick-accurate or suitable for live trading decisions.

Your broker's own quoted price is always the authoritative source for trade execution. Discrepancies between our display and your broker's quotes are normal.

SECTION 08

Jurisdiction-specific notes

UK users: CFDs and spread bets are restricted-leverage instruments. The FCA has published consumer warnings on retail CFD trading. You can read the latest at fca.org.uk.

US users: Retail spot FX is regulated by the NFA / CFTC. Many CFD-style products available to overseas traders are not permitted for US persons. Verify with your broker before opening a position.

EU users: ESMA leverage limits apply. The 70-85% retail loss statistic referenced above is published by ESMA-regulated brokers on their own websites.

Australia: ASIC has imposed product intervention measures on retail CFDs since March 2021. Leverage and marketing restrictions apply.

Singapore: CFDs are restricted to accredited investors in most cases under MAS rules.

If TradeContext.ai is not legally available in your jurisdiction or for your account type, do not use it. We accept no liability for use outside its intended scope.
