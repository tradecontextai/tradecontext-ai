❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about TradeContext.ai. Still have questions? Email us →
Is there a free trial?+
No free trial — TradeContext.ai is a premium platform paid from day one. You get a 2-minute free preview on the Dashboard and News Bias Feed so you can see exactly what you're getting before subscribing.
What's the difference between Pro and Elite?+
Pro gives you full access to the dashboard, live news bias feed, AI playbooks, markets & charts, broker hub, watchlist and alerts. Elite adds the AI Trading Journal and Funded Account Tracker — essential if you're running a prop firm challenge.
How does the news bias feed work?+
Our AI reads breaking financial news in real time and scores each story as bullish, bearish, or neutral for the affected assets. You see the bias before price reacts — giving you time to position accordingly.
Which brokers can I connect?+
We support MT4, MT5, OANDA, Binance, Interactive Brokers and Coinbase. More integrations are being added. All connections are read-only — we can never move your funds.
What funded account firms do you support?+
Currently supported: FTMO, The5%ers, MyForexFunds, TopStep, True Forex Funds and Instant Funding. The tracker monitors your drawdown limits, daily loss rules and profit targets in real time.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes — cancel anytime from your account settings. No lock-ins, no questions asked. For billing queries email hello@tradecontext.ai.
Is my data secure?+
All broker API connections are read-only. Your credentials are encrypted with AES-256. We never share your data with third parties.
