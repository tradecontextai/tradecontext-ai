Why we built this

Trading platforms show you charts. We tell you what they mean.

The problem

Every retail trader stares at the same charts. The same indicators. The same TradingView feed. Yet the people moving the market — institutional desks, central bank traders, prop shops — are reading news, macro flow and order book context in real time and acting on it before retail even reads the headline.

That asymmetry is the gap between losing and winning. We built TradeContext.ai to close it.
