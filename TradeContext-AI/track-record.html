How we score

For every bias the AI prints, we snapshot the live price at generation. 1h, 4h and 24h later we re-fetch the price and ask: did it move in the predicted direction? "Bullish" hits when price > gen-price 24h later (vice-versa for bearish). "Neutral" calls are excluded from the hit rate (no direction to check). We never edit the dataset — every call is permanent the moment it's generated.
